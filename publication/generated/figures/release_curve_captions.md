## Figure S1. Dolpap / C1E

Figure S1. Force-displacement release curves for Dolpap liner tested with C1E butyl sealant. Panel (a) shows the outer liner side (n = 10 shown from 10 traces); panel (b) shows the inner liner side (n = 10 shown from 10 traces). Thin blue lines show individual replicates and the dark blue line shows the mean curve where replicate coverage was sufficient. Forces are normalized to cN/25 mm; the displayed range corresponds to the 50-200 mm analysis interval. Both panels use the shared 0-30 cN/25 mm comparison y-axis.
