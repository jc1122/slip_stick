# Supplementary Force-Displacement Curve Figures

The following figures accompany the manuscript as Figures S1-S21. Each figure contains the external and internal side of one liner-sealant pair.

## Figure S1. Dolpap / C1E

Figure S1. Force-displacement release curves for Dolpap liner tested with C1E butyl sealant. Panel (a) shows the external liner side (n = 10 shown from 10 traces); panel (b) shows the internal liner side (n = 10 shown from 10 traces). Thin blue lines show individual replicates and the dark blue line shows the mean curve where replicate coverage was sufficient. Forces are normalized to cN/25 mm; the displayed range corresponds to the 50-200 mm analysis interval. Both panels use the shared 0-30 cN/25 mm comparison y-axis.

PNG: `supplementary_material/figures/png/S01_dolpap_C1E_release_curves.png`

PDF: `supplementary_material/figures/pdf/S01_dolpap_C1E_release_curves.pdf`

## Figure S2. Dolpap / U2E

Figure S2. Force-displacement release curves for Dolpap liner tested with U2E butyl sealant. The external liner side (n = 10 shown from 10 traces) is shown as paired 0-30 comparison and full-range panels; the internal liner side (n = 10 shown from 10 traces) is shown as one 0-30 comparison panel. Thin blue lines show individual replicates and the dark blue line shows the mean curve where replicate coverage was sufficient. Forces are normalized to cN/25 mm; the displayed range corresponds to the 50-200 mm analysis interval. Panels marked as 0-30 comparison use a shared 0-30 cN/25 mm y-axis, with values above this range omitted from that comparison view. Full-range panels retain all above-range values. Sides with more than 5% of samples above 30 cN/25 mm are shown only at full range (figure maximum 81.4 cN/25 mm).

PNG: `supplementary_material/figures/png/S02_dolpap_U2E_release_curves.png`

PDF: `supplementary_material/figures/pdf/S02_dolpap_U2E_release_curves.pdf`
